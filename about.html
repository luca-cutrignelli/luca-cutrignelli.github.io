Index Luca Cutrignelli About

Luca Cutrignelli (b. 1999, Bari, Italy) is an editor working between Milan and Rome, with a sharp eye for storytelling through the cut.

He started out at Sky Italia as Edit Producer and Scriptwriter for on-air channels TV8 and Cielo (2021–2022), sharpening his visual language in television storytelling.

Now working as a Freelance Editor, he's brought his craft to international spots for brands including Lamborghini, Harper's Bazaar Korea x Prada, Trussardi, MG Automotive, McLaren, Otto d'Ame, and Ermanno Scervino.
